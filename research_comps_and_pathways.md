### Why 22159 underwrites differently than the SFR comps
1. **MF zoning baseline = 8 units** carries forward under no-net-loss + Ord 524 — not replicable on a raw SFR lot
2. **0.45 ac** is materially larger than most PCH burn lots transacting (avg 2.9–5K SF), giving you bulk and unit-count flexibility
3. **Inland-side** removes seawall, wave-action, public-access-easement risk that's compressing oceanfront comps
4. The right buyer is a **rental-MF or for-sale-condo developer**, not the luxury-SFR investor pool absorbing oceanfront — a different bid, often tighter $/door

### Implied land bracket for 22159
- Floor: $1.3M (Malibu median lot)
- Mid: $2.5–3.5M based on **$/SF land** triangulation (19,612 SF × ~$150–180/SF inland-MF distressed = $2.9–3.5M)
- Upside: $4M+ if a developer underwrites the **density + ADU stack** (see §3 below)

The **$3.0M land / $9M finished** anchor in the current BOV is **defensible and probably conservative** if the entitlement story is fully developed.

## 2. Rebuild pathways — what a developer can actually build

The legal stack governing the rebuild (in order of operations):
- **City of Malibu Ordinance 524** — disaster rebuild ordinance, adopted 3/12/25, certified by Coastal Commission 4/10/25
- **Governor's Executive Orders N-4-25 / N-20-25** — CEQA & Coastal Act suspensions, 30-day permit goal
- **LCP Amendment (Apr 2025)** — codified CDP exemptions/waivers for disaster replacement structures
- **SB 330 / Housing Crisis Act** — no-net-loss protection (8-unit baseline preserved)
- **ZTA-25-003** (pending) — raises detached-ADU cap on multifamily lots from 2 → **8**

### Pathway A — Like-for-like + 10% (Ord 524 "exemption" track) ✅ FASTEST
| | |
|---|---|
| Envelope | Same 8 units, same footprint, up to **110% of original** in SF / height / bulk → up to ~**5,592 SF**, modest height bump |
| Approval | Planning Verification form **or** Admin Plan Review |
| Fees | from **$200** |
| Timeline | **1 week – 6 months** |
| CDP | **Exempt** (LCP disaster-replacement carve-out) |
| Best for | Owner-rebuild or operator looking to flip permits fast |

### Pathway B — Full CDP / expanded redesign (>110%)
| | |
|---|---|
| Envelope | Footprint relocation, larger SF, new layout; still capped by underlying MF/MFBF density unless stacked with bonuses |
| Approval | Coastal Development Permit |
| Fees | base planning fee **~$11,579** |
| Timeline | **12–24 months**, public hearing |
| Best for | Developer wanting a luxury rebuild or ground-up redesign of 8 condo-style units |

### Pathway C — 8 units + detached ADUs (density stack) 🔼 HIGHEST UPSIDE
- Under current code: **2 detached ADUs** allowed on a multifamily lot
- Under pending **ZTA-25-003**: cap rises to **8 detached ADUs** → potential **8 base + 8 ADU = 16 doors**
- Each ADU: CDP on **60-day clock**, no public hearing, no Coastal Commission appeal
- 4 ft side/rear setbacks; **1 parking space per ADU** (with multifamily/transit exemptions)
- Attached ADUs currently barred on MF lots; ZTA-25-003 removes that bar too
- **This is the story to tell the buyer** — if the 0.45 ac can physically site 8 detached ADUs (need site plan check), the doors-count nearly doubles without losing the Ord 524 fast track

### Pathway D — State Density Bonus overlay
- Add an affordable set-aside (15–20% LIHTC) → bonus units + concessions
- Could push **base 8 → 10–12 units** with affordability
- Stackable with ADU path in principle; needs counsel + Malibu Planning sign-off
- Useful as a back-pocket lever, less likely to be the lead pitch in Malibu

### Pathway E — Convert MF → SFR ❌ NOT RECOMMENDED
- No-net-loss requires ADU replacement for every unit dropped → defeats the simplification
- Loses the MF/MFBF zoning entitlement premium that's the entire reason the lot underwrites at MF land $/SF
- Only makes sense if a single ultra-luxury owner-user pays a premium for the lot as a teardown SFR — possible in this corridor, but a different buyer profile

### Beachfront / seaward-of-PCH note
- If the parcel turns out to be seaward of PCH (verify with a coastal-zone map — APN 4451-008 typically maps inland but confirm): **FEMA flood zone** rules apply; **no full wave-action report** needed for a replacement structure (vs. new construction). Confirm Base Flood Elevation in early design.

## 3. Recommended buyer pitch (BOV refinement)

Lead with three numbers:
1. **8-unit MF entitlement preserved** under no-net-loss + Ord 524 (fast track)
2. **Up to 16 doors** via ADU stack if ZTA-25-003 passes (track the hearing date in the BOV)
3. **30-day permit goal** under EO N-4-25 — uniquely fast for the coastal zone

Then the comp triangulation:
- Inland-MF burn-lot $/SF: ~$150–180 → $2.9–3.5M land
- ADU upside: developer underwrites $/door, not $/SF land — supports $3.5–4M+ for the right buyer
